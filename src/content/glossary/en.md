# AAL (Average Annual Loss)

The expected economic damage per year, averaged over all simulated events.
AAL is used to compare the overall risk between different hazards, countries,
or climate scenarios in a single number.

# Adaptation Measure

A physical, structural, or behavioural action that reduces the impact of a
climate hazard. Examples include sea walls, early-warning systems, and
improved building codes.

# Climate Scenario

A plausible future climate trajectory based on assumptions about emissions
and socio-economic development (see also SSP). Common scenarios include
RCP 2.6, RCP 4.5, RCP 6.0, and RCP 8.5.

# Cost-Benefit Ratio (CBR)

The monetary benefits of an adaptation measure divided by its cost. A CBR
greater than 1 indicates that the measure is economically profitable over
its lifetime.

# CRED

Climate Risk Economic Damage – the macroeconomic output dataset used in
RISK WISE to explore how hazards and adaptation affect country-level GDP,
consumption, and employment.

# ERA

Enhancing Risk Assessments – the GIZ / UNU-EHS project that produced the
calibrated datasets for Egypt and Thailand that ship with RISK WISE in
ERA mode.

# Exceedance Probability

The probability that an event of a given magnitude or greater will occur in
any given year. It is the inverse of the return period.

# Exposure

The people, infrastructure, economic assets, or ecosystems that could be
affected by a hazard. In RISK WISE, exposure is represented spatially as
a map of asset values or population counts.

# Hazard

A natural phenomenon that can cause loss or damage, such as floods,
tropical cyclones, wildfires, droughts, earthquakes, or heatwaves.

# Impact

The consequence of a hazard event on exposed assets, expressed as economic
damage, affected population, or another damage metric. Impact equals the
convolution of hazard intensity, exposure, and the impact function.

# Impact Function

A curve that relates the intensity of a hazard (e.g. wind speed, water
depth) to the fraction of exposure value that is damaged. Also called a
vulnerability function or damage function.

# LitPop

A global exposure dataset that disaggregates national asset value to the
pixel level using nightlight intensity and population. Used as a proxy for
economic value where detailed asset maps are unavailable.

# Probabilistic Assessment

A risk assessment that considers the full distribution of possible events
and their probabilities, rather than a single deterministic scenario.
RISK WISE runs probabilistic assessments via CLIMADA.

# RCP (Representative Concentration Pathway)

A greenhouse-gas concentration trajectory used by the IPCC. RCP 2.6 is a
strong mitigation pathway, while RCP 8.5 is a high-emissions baseline.

# Return Period

The average interval between events of a given magnitude, in years. A
"100-year flood" has a 1 % exceedance probability in any given year – it
does not mean such a flood occurs only once per century.

# Risk

The combination of hazard, exposure, and vulnerability. In quantitative
terms, risk is the expected impact integrated over all possible events.

# Scenario

A combination of climate scenario (e.g. RCP) and socio-economic scenario
(e.g. SSP) used to describe a plausible future state. In RISK WISE, the
scenario selector pins both together.

# Sector

An economic activity grouping (agriculture, manufacturing, tourism, etc.)
used in the macroeconomic view to filter CRED outputs.

# SSP (Shared Socioeconomic Pathway)

A global narrative of future socio-economic development. RISK WISE uses
SSPs to scale exposure forward in time alongside RCP climate scenarios.

# Time Horizon

The future year or year range for which risk is evaluated. Typical values
in RISK WISE range from 2024 (present) up to 2100.

# Vulnerability

The propensity of exposed assets to be harmed by a hazard. Captured
quantitatively by the impact function.

# Waterfall Chart

A visualization that decomposes the change in risk between a present-day
baseline and a future scenario into incremental contributions from
hazard, exposure, and adaptation.
